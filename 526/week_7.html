Pandas

You may notice this week that we are using the Pandas library to load our datasets

Data plays a huge role in machine learning, so it is not sufficient to understand how to train models alone, you also need to understand how to work with and manipulate data

This is where Pandas comes in, a very good, widely used data library built upon numpy. Knowledge of Pandas will help greatly in your machine learning journey

That is where this weeks guided learning comes in. I would like you to skim through the W3Schools tutorial on Pandas. At the very least you should try to get a sense of the things that you can do with Pandas.
