Lab 2 - Task

Now it's time for you to take a more open ended project to really get a feel for fuzzy logic:

A company has turned over a new leaf! Recently, HR has noticed a rise in burnout cases and absenteeism. While traditional surveys offer some insight, expecting managers to allocate work dynamically without guidance is challenging!

To address this, the company wants to develop a fuzzy logic-based system that estimates how much workload (i.e. number of projects/tasks) an employee should undertake at a specific time using measurable inputs. You are asked to prototype this system.

You'll notice this is open to interpretation, that's because I want you to think about how to put this system together rather than simply following pre-provided instructions. You should consider:

  • You know the consequent is workload (that the employee can be allocated). You will need to design the universe of discourse for this (membership etc.)
  • You will need to come up with 2 antecedents and design the universe of discourse for them.
    You only need 2 for this task (that will spare you a lot of work for the next point). However, you should try and come up with ones that could reasonably be measured by a company (things like hours of sleep would be inappropriate)
  • You should consider what you want the rules to be
  • Should you finish early, and you've tested your fuzzy system, consider adding more antecedents and updating your rules
Hints: Current task load, Commute time, weeks since they last took annual leave, Coffee orders, meetings per week, email volume per day, overtime hours this week, Number of deadlines this week
